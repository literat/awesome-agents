# /eli5 - Explain It Like I'm Five

Explain the given term or concept as simply as possible

## Usage

```
/eli5 [term or concept]
```

## What to do

Explain the given term or concept as simply as possible, in the spirit of "explain it so that even a 5-year-old would understand it".
Do not use technical jargon.
Explain relevant related terms.
Give at least two simple and concrete examples that are as close to real life situations as possible, as long as the concept is still easy to understand.
